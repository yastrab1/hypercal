# HyperCal

## Register

product

## Users

A student planning school and personal time on a desktop keyboard and an Android phone. Frequent use requires quick navigation and clear separation of detailed timetables from longer-term commitments.

## Product Purpose

A Google Calendar interface with day, week, month and year navigation, iCalendar subscriptions, and saved filtered views. Google remains the source of truth for Google events. Custom views change visibility without changing underlying events.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, familiar, efficient. The user approved a Google Calendar inspired interface and specifically requested excellent keyboard support like Todoist and Notion Calendar.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, unnecessary cards, and hiding essential actions behind pointer-only controls.

## Design Principles

- Calendar content takes priority over chrome.
- Reach routine actions from the keyboard.
- Views filter data without deleting it.
- Be explicit about demo data, connection state, and failed writes.
- Mobile and web support everyday viewing, event edits, saved-view composition, and plugin settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable system typography, keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic controls, reduced motion support, text labels alongside calendar colors.
